# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Designers (and design-minded engineers) browsing Harikrishnan's portfolio. They arrive curious, skim quickly, and judge craft within seconds. On `/archive` they should come away impressed by the history: a visible record of how the site evolved, version by version.

## Product Purpose
A personal portfolio for Harikrishnan V K, a full-stack developer. The `/archive` route is a personal Wayback Machine: the live site first, then every earlier build, browsable and comparable side by side. Success is a designer thinking "this person is cool, look at all this history" and exploring several versions.

## Brand Personality
Warm, personal, nostalgic. A time capsule rather than a changelog. Quiet confidence in the chrome so the old builds are the show; small, considered details reward a closer look.

## Anti-references
- Drifting away from the existing design system. Use the current tokens, fonts and components, and clean them up rather than inventing a new look.
- Clutter: redundant labels, badges, counters and chrome that repeat what the content already says.

## Design Principles
1. The history is the hero. Previews of real builds carry the page; UI chrome stays out of their way.
2. Say it once. Every date, title and control appears in one clear place.
3. Nostalgia through restraint. Warmth comes from the content and small touches, not decoration.
4. Same system, cleaner. Extend existing tokens and patterns; remove drift instead of adding new styles.
5. Feels good in the hand. Swipes, taps and drags respond immediately on mobile and desktop.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA. Full keyboard support for the timeline, carousel and compare slider; visible focus; 44px touch targets on mobile; every animation has a reduced-motion alternative.
